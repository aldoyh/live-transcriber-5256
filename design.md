# Frequency design system
Reference: selected music artist site, used as visual inspiration only. Translate its near-black canvas, saturated green accents, oversized uppercase typography, photographic square artwork and thin rules into an editorial podcast discovery interface.

Palette: background #111312, sidebar #0c0e0d, surface #1b1e1c, muted #898e89, ink #f4f5ee, electric green #b5f55c. Green duotone photography for the featured editorial banner.
Typography: Poppins body, condensed bold display using Barlow Condensed. Small uppercase mono labels for metadata. Headings tight and confident; body airy and legible.
Layout: fixed 224px navigation on desktop, 108px persistent bottom player, content max width 1500px with 40px gutters. 340px feature banner with hard edges; category pills, four-column episode grid, compact horizontal trending cards. Mobile collapses navigation to top menu and bottom player, two columns or horizontally scrolling rows.
Components: outline icon buttons, green primary buttons, subtle square corners, thin charcoal borders. Photography and original editorial typography compose podcast covers. Hover artwork play controls, saved toggles, searchable catalog, category filtering, episode detail drawer.
UX: local saved shows and history, real HTML audio with sample narration, seek, speed, mute and skip. No authentication, backend or database. All browsing data curated locally. Navigation selects Discover, Browse, Saved and History.
